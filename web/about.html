Chapter 1

System Overview

VoucherPalace is a universal voucher platform that enables companies AND individuals to create, distribute, and track digital vouchers, while allowing merchants to redeem them and users to utilize their vouchers seamlessly.

🎯 Key Differentiator: Unlike traditional voucher systems that only allow companies to issue vouchers, VoucherPalace empowers anyone to become a voucher issuer — whether you're a multinational corporation or an individual giving a birthday gift.

Companies

Bulk Issuer

Create and manage large-scale voucher campaigns, track performance, and analyze redemption data.

  • Issue 1,000+ vouchers at once
  • Set value, expiry, and merchant restrictions
  • View redemption analytics & reports
  • Assign to specific users or open campaigns

Individuals

Personal Issuer

Create gift vouchers, rewards, or personal promotions — no company account required.

  • Issue single or multiple vouchers
  • Perfect for birthdays, gifts, or small businesses
  • Auto-generate secure 10-digit codes
  • No minimum issue requirements

Merchants

Accept and validate vouchers from both companies AND individuals.

  • Real-time voucher validation
  • Instant redemption processing
  • Transaction history & daily reports
  • Accept vouchers from any issuer

Users

Receive and redeem vouchers at participating merchants.

  • View available vouchers
  • Check voucher status in real-time
  • Track redemption history
  • Receive vouchers from companies or friends

Chapter 7

For Merchants

Redeeming Vouchers

Quick Redemption Guide:

  1. 1 Click Redeem Voucher button
  2. 2 Enter the 10-digit voucher code
  3. 3 System auto-fills voucher details — shows issuer (Company or Individual)
  4. 4 Select the redeeming user
  5. 5 Click Confirm Redemption

Validation Rules

System automatically checks:

  • Voucher exists in system
  • Voucher status is ACTIVE
  • Not expired (valid expiry date)
  • Redemptions under max limit
  • User is valid and active
  • Merchants can redeem vouchers from BOTH companies AND individuals

Chapter 13

Troubleshooting

Voucher not found

Ensure the code is entered exactly as shown, including the VP- prefix. Codes are case-sensitive.

Voucher expired

Check the expiry date. Expired vouchers cannot be redeemed. Contact the issuer (company or individual).

Cannot issue voucher as individual

Make sure you're logged in with a personal account (not a company account). The same form works for both!

Chapter 14

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a company to issue vouchers?

No! Individuals can issue vouchers immediately after signing up. Perfect for gifts, small businesses, or personal use.

How are voucher codes generated?

Voucher codes can be manually entered or auto-generated with the format VP-XXXXXXXXXX (10 random digits). Click the 🎲 dice button!

Can I issue multiple vouchers as an individual?

Yes! You can issue as many vouchers as you want, one at a time. Each gets a unique code automatically.

What's the difference between company and individual accounts?

Companies get bulk issuance, analytics, merchant restrictions, and API access. Individuals get instant issuance with auto-generated codes — perfect for gifts.

Can merchants see who issued the voucher?

Yes, the redemption screen shows whether a voucher was issued by a company or an individual, along with the issuer name.

How do I get support?

Email us at support@voucherpalace.com or use the contact form in your dashboard.
